The generated land particles use Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons (public domain).

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Regenerate with `node scripts/generate-globe-land.mjs path/to/ne_110m_land.geojson`.
Point brightness is procedural art direction, not population or city data.
